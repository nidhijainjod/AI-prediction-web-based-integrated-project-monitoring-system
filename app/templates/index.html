Platform

Everything a PMO needs to catch risk early

One model, trained on real project-stage dynamics, powering every screen — from the manager's task board to the administrator's portfolio dashboard.

Predictive Cost Modeling

A trained regression model projects final cost from current spend pace, catching budget creep long before close-out.

Schedule Delay Forecasting

Elapsed time vs. planned pace, blocked tasks and overdue work are combined into a predicted completion date.

0–100 Risk Scoring

Every project gets a single Low / Medium / High / Critical score so leadership can triage in seconds.

Explainable AI

No black box — each score ships with the top drivers in plain English, ranked by model feature importance.

Corrective Recommendations

Each risk driver maps to a specific recommended action — escalate blockers, re-baseline budget, compress the critical path.

Role-Based Workspaces

Managers get full project & task CRUD; administrators get read-only portfolio oversight and user management.
